Welcome to Veridia

Public money has vanished while roads crumble and schools fall apart. You are an independent anti-corruption investigator. Build a case before time runs out, then put recovered money to work.

  • Move with WASD or arrow keys.
  • Walk near a glowing marker and press E.
  • Collect all 3 evidence items before making an accusation.
  • Arrest only the politician supported by the evidence, then fund 3 city projects.

Mission briefing

Investigate three leads and submit a fair, evidence-based case against the right official.

Your investigation begins when you press “Start investigation.”
